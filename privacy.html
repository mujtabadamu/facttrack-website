FactTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

FactTrack is a Chrome extension that reads video captions on pages you visit and checks factual claims against live sources. This page explains what data it collects, why, and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Video captions. When FactTrack is turned on, it reads the captions already showing on a video and sends short rolling windows of that text to our server to detect and verify factual claims. We do not store the caption text or the claims themselves after processing — only summary metadata (claim type, verdict, the site's domain, and a timestamp) is kept, so we can show you your usage history and keep the service running reliably.
  • Account info, if you sign in. Signing in with Google shares your email address and profile picture with us, used to identify your account, raise your free-check limit past the anonymous cap, and manage your subscription if you have one. We never see your Google password.
  • A device identifier. The extension generates a random ID (not tied to your hardware) and stores it locally, used to enforce the free anonymous-usage limit before you sign in.
  • A hashed IP address, for anonymous use only. As a backup abuse-prevention signal, we store a one-way hash of your IP address — never the IP itself — for requests made before you sign in.
  • Payment information, if you subscribe. Payments are handled entirely by Paystack. We never see or store your card details — only your subscription status is shared back with us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't store the text of captions or claims after they're checked.
  • We don't track your browsing beyond the usage summary described above.

Third parties we use

To run FactTrack, the data above passes through a small set of service providers, each only for what they're named for:

  • Google — sign-in
  • Supabase — account and usage data storage
  • DeepSeek — AI processing of caption text to detect and verify claims
  • Paystack — subscription payment processing
  • Railway — hosting for our server

Your choices

You can use FactTrack anonymously without ever signing in. You can turn checking off at any time from the extension popup. To request that your account and usage data be deleted, email us at mujtabadamu@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

FactTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
